The Lupo app

Lupo collects no personal data. The app has no account, no server of its own, no cloud sync, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. Your music, markings, recordings and settings stay on your iPad and are never sent to me.

  • Camera: to scan paper music and, if you turn it on, to turn pages with head or mouth movements. Images are processed on the device only and are neither stored nor sent.
  • Microphone: for the tuner and your practice recordings. Recordings are stored on your iPad only.
  • Bluetooth and local network: for pedals, MIDI devices, the Apple Watch remote and the stand partner. Connections go directly from device to device, without a server.
  • Purchases: Lupo Premium is bought through Apple. I receive no payment data. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • Crash reports: if you agreed in iPadOS Settings, Apple shares anonymised crash and usage data with developers. You control this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.
