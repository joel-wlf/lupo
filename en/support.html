Questions

How do I get my music into Lupo?

Drag PDFs in from the Files app, open them from Mail or Safari with Lupo, or scan paper music with the camera.

How do I set up my pedal?

Open Settings in Lupo and press your pedal once in the pedal section. Lupo remembers the key.

Where is my music stored?

As regular PDF files in Lupo's folder, visible in the Files app under On My iPad. Lupo also makes an automatic backup on your iPad every day.

I bought Lupo Premium but don't see it.

Open the purchase sheet and tap Restore Purchases. The purchase belongs to your Apple Account and is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
